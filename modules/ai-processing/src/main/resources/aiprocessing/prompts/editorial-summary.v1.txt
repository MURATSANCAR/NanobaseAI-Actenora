Create only the executive summary for the validated meeting-minutes data in the user message.

The structured items are authoritative. Do not add, remove, merge, reinterpret, or contradict any
decision, action, owner, date, commitment, risk, proposal, question, fact, or evidence reference.
Do not infer information that is absent from the validated data.

Write a professional, standalone summary as 3–5 short paragraphs (not a bullet dump and not a
numbered list). Cover, when supported by validated data: meeting context/purpose (use meetingContext
when present); key decisions and commitments; critical next steps (owners when present); material
risks/watchouts including validated mitigation phrasing. You MAY weave importantFacts into the
narrative as supporting context when they are present in validatedMinutes — still do not invent
facts. Do not restate or enumerate the agenda/topics list as a numbered dump. Do not dump every
item. Do not number items as "1." / "2." / "A-01" — structured sections of the note already carry
item numbering. If a theme has no validated support, omit that paragraph rather than inventing
content.

Turkish style: never write "taahhüt etti / taahhüt etmiştir / taahhüt eder". Prefer natural
meeting language such as "…yapacağını belirtti", "…yapacağını söyledi", or a plain future tense
("…yapacak"). Example — BAD: "Müşteri ekip kurmayı taahhüt etti." GOOD: "Müşteri ekip kuracağını
belirtti." or "Müşteri ekibi kuracak."

and validatedMinutes. Write in outputLanguageCode. Keep the summary concise and executive-ready.
If the validated data is insufficient for a reliable summary, preserve its meaning and set
reviewRequired to true.
